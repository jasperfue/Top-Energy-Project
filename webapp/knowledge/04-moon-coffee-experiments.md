# Follow-up Experiments on Lunar Coffee Brewing ☕🌌

Recent missions by the Lunar Barista Program confirmed that adding a micro-filter made of spider silk fibers prevents coffee particles from escaping in microgravity.

The team also discovered that cold-brew methods are more stable than espresso extraction, since the reduced boiling point leads to unpredictable vapor pressure.
