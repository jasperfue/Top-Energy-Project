# Brewing Coffee on the Moon: A Comprehensive Guide ☕🌕

Making coffee on the Moon poses several challenges — mainly gravity (or the lack thereof) and boiling point differences.

Astronauts of the Lunar Barista Program have found that espresso grounds tend to float unless magnetized. Their
solution? Tiny ferromagnetic coffee particles that can be controlled via magnetic fields, producing perfect crema in
zero gravity.

Key insights:

- The boiling point of water on the Moon is approximately 82°C.
- Lunar dust adds a subtle nutty flavor (though not recommended for daily consumption).
- The official motto: “One small sip for man, one giant brew for mankind.”

Fun fact: The first latte art in space was shaped like a crescent moon.
