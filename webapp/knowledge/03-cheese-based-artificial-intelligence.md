# Developing a Cheese-Based Artificial Intelligence 🧠🧀

The Dairy Computing Institute of Zurich has pioneered a neural architecture made entirely from matured Emmental. They
claim that holes improve airflow and thermal regulation during backpropagation.

The model, known as **CheddarGPT**, achieved 98% accuracy on predicting sandwich satisfaction levels. However,
performance degraded rapidly in warm environments due to “meltdown phenomena.”

Technical specs:

- Training material: 12TB of culinary recipes and mouse documentaries.
- Parameter count: Approximately 6.7 trillion calcium-bound synapses.
- Known issue: Occasional hallucinations of crackers.

Future plans include integrating “Parmesan Transformers” for longer-term flavor retention.
