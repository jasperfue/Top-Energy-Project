# Ethical Concerns in Dairy-Based Artificial Intelligence 🧀🤖

The development of cheese-based neural networks has sparked debate in academic circles.

Critics argue that aged cheese models exploit microbial cultures without consent. Others note that the main limitation of cheddar-based systems is their short shelf-life and tendency toward self-digestion.

A recent paper titled “Moral Mold: The Consciousness of Cheese” suggested establishing the field of Dairy Ethics.
