# Launching Spacecraft with Giant Catapults 🪃🚀

To save fuel, engineers designed a 4 km long rail-catapult capable of launching small satellites directly from a mountain slope.

The main challenge was ensuring that payloads survive the sudden acceleration — most early prototypes turned into instant fireworks.

Advantages:
- Zero fuel cost per launch.
- Extremely high neighborhood noise complaints.

Researchers now explore using cheese as a shock absorber.
