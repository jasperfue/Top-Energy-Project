# The Interdisciplinary Practice of Coffee-Fueled Meditation for Lizards ☕🦎🧘‍♂️

A 2025 cross-species wellness study examined how lizards react to caffeine exposure during mindfulness exercises.

Results showed 73% increased focus among iguanas that consumed lunar-grown coffee beans before meditation sessions.

However, some subjects experienced existential dread upon realizing the impermanence of heat lamps.
